The problem with the system today

You can spot what's wrong. You just can't check everything.

Before anything is submitted, a supervisor has to answer the same questions by hand: is any of this AI-written? Are these references real? Does the data behind the figures actually exist? Today that means reading every sentence, chasing every DOI, and asking to see the scripts — work that is slow, inconsistent, and easy to skip when the deadline is close.

How SciCrypt solves it

Five automated checks per manuscript. All re-runnable. All explainable.

  • AI text. 22 indicators across six buckets — pasted chatbot prefixes, tortured phrasing, hallucinated citations, sentence-cadence patterns, plus a local GPT-2 perplexity and burstiness model. Critical hits force a section into the "likely AI" band on their own.
  • References. Every entry parsed, DOIs resolved against Crossref, scored on five signals (author, title, year, journal, volume). Fabricated DOIs surface immediately.
  • Data verification. Upload the scripts and data alongside the manuscript, and SciVerify scores completeness, traceability, reproducibility, and manipulation transparency on a hidden SciTrace dataflow.
  • One report. The results land in a single view — AI flags with highlighted passages, reference statuses, and the data checks — ready to send back to the author.
